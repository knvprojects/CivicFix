How CivicFix Works

A transparent, data-driven pipeline from citizen reporting to municipal resolution.

📸

1. Report with Photo & AI

Citizens upload on-site photos and descriptions. Built-in AI suggests the right municipal category with human confirmation.

🔍

2. Duplicate Detection

Proximity matching and text token analysis identify duplicate reports nearby, consolidating citizen voice instead of cluttering tickets.

⚖️

3. Priority & Auto-Routing

Dynamic scoring formula factors severity, affected population, report age, and duplicate counts to dispatch to the correct department.

⏱️

4. SLA Escalations & Tracking

Every ticket has an immutable timeline. Delays automatically trigger escalation reminders for responsible representatives.

Explore Role-Based Workflows

CivicFix supports four primary stakeholder roles. Test each workflow instantly:

👤 Citizen Report & Track

Lodge complaints, upload pictures, receive status timeline notifications, and track resolution.

🛡️ Issue Moderator Verify & Triage

Verify ground reports, review duplicate candidate tickets, merge reports, and route to departments.

🏢 Department Rep Resolve & Update

View department work queues, update status to 'In Progress', and mark tickets 'Resolved' with notes.

⚙️ Administrator Oversee & Audit

Supervise municipal health, monitor overdue SLA escalations, analyze historical recurring hotspots.
